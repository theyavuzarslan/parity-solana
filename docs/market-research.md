Two ways to state a move, and they give different numbers. **USD/TRY rise** = new/old − 1. **Lira loss of dollar value** = 1 − old/new. The pitch uses **lira loss** throughout.

Pitch-safe lines:
- "In two weeks of August 2018 the lira lost 29% of its dollar value." (TCMB series above)
- "In 2021 it lost 43%." (TCMB series; Reuters reported 44% on a slightly different window: https://gulfnews.com/business/banking/turkeys-lira-logs-worst-year-in-two-decades-1.84692293)
- "A dollar bought 3.77 lira at the end of 2017. Last Friday it bought 49.13." (TCMB)
- The December 2021 reversal matters for the demo story: rates can also snap the other way within days, so a hedge has to survive both directions.

Supporting press (not used for numbers): CRS summary of the 2018 crisis, https://legistorm.com/reports/view/crs/322564/Turkey_s_Currency_Crisis.html ; Bloomberg 10 Aug 2018, https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2018-08-10/turkey-crisis-in-charts-worse-than-lehman-similar-to-1998 .

- **~$200 billion** of annual crypto value received in Turkey, the largest market in MENA, about four times the UAE ($53B). Chainalysis 2025 MENA report: https://www.chainalysis.com/blog/middle-east-north-africa-crypto-adoption-2025/
- Same report: about $878 billion of gross crypto inflows to Turkey from early 2021 to mid-2025; it links growth to lira depreciation that "began accelerating in Q1 2021". It also says altcoin trading overtook stablecoin trading on Turkish exchanges in late 2024–2025, and retail segments contracted. (Honest framing: the 2025 boom is partly speculative.) Commentary: https://cointelegraph.com/news/turkey-s-200b-crypto-boom-is-built-on-speculation-not-adoption-chainalysis
- **Stablecoin purchases = 4.3% of GDP**, the highest of any economy covered, for **April 2023 – March 2024** (≈$38B of TRY↔stablecoin swaps vs 2022 GDP). Chainalysis via Cointelegraph: https://cointelegraph.com/news/stablecoin-buys-turkey-4-percent-gdp ; Axios: https://www.axios.com/2024/04/26/turkey-stablecoin-spending-reaches-4-of-gdp-crypto . - **UNVERIFIED:** "55% of value received in Turkey is stablecoins" (secondary, period unclear). Do not use.

## 4. USD/TRY futures on Borsa Istanbul (VİOP)

What exists:
- USD/TRY futures and options trade on VİOP (Borsa Istanbul Futures and Options Market). Contract size **USD 1,000**; tick TRY 0.0001 per dollar = TRY 0.1 per contract. Source: https://borsaistanbul.com/files/VIOP-fx-derivatives.pdf
- Six contracts trade at once: current month, next month, next three even-month cycle months (Feb/Apr/Jun/Aug/Oct/Dec) and December. Source (2020 circular): https://www.borsaistanbul.com/files/2020-49_USDTRY_Futures_Cycle_Month_Change.pdf
- USD/TRY options also exist, USD 1,000 size: https://www.borsaistanbul.com/files/VIOP_TRY_USD_Option_Contracts.pdf
- Access is through a licensed Turkish intermediary (aracı kurum); initial margin is posted to Takasbank; margin calls apply. Source: broker guides, e.g. https://www.gedik.com/hizmetler/viop , https://paratic.com/rehber/viop/viopta-nasil-islem-yapilir/

**UNVERIFIED:** the current per-contract initial margin for USD/TRY, account minimums, and whether non-residents (e.g. a Turkish freelancer living abroad) can open a VİOP account. Brokers publish margins but we did not find a current table.

Related regulation: since 10 Feb 2017 the CMB (SPK) caps retail margin trading at 10:1 and requires a TRY 50,000 minimum deposit for that product. Source: https://spk.gov.tr/duyurular/basin-duyurulari/2017/yatirim-hizmetleri-ve-faaliyetleri-ile-yan-hizmetlere-iliskin-esaslar-hakkinda-tebligde-iii-37-1-degisiklik-yapilmasina-dair-teblig-hakkinda . Parity's 10% minimum initial margin is set to sit inside that cap. Whether the 2017 rule applies to Parity at all is a legal question we have not answered. (Product copy must not name this concept; see PRODUCT.md copy rules.)

How Parity differs from VİOP (claims we can defend): positions from $10 instead of $1,000 contracts; any tenor from 1 to 365 days instead of fixed expiries; stablecoin margin and settlement; the forward price is computed in the open from the rate gap. What we cannot claim: that VİOP is unavailable to our users (unverified), or that Parity is cheaper (no live fee comparison).

The structural difference, which is the pitch line: perpetuals have no maturity and are kept near spot by a funding rate that changes every hour, so the cost of holding a hedge for 90 days is unknown on day one. A dated forward fixes the rate and the cost at the start, and its price comes from the published interest-rate gap. Phrase the gap as "**we did not find a USD/TRY or TRY-cross listing on any of these venues**", never "none exists".
